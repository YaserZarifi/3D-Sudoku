## 3D Sudoku 1.1.0

This update is about making the game easier to pick up and nicer to play.

### A proper welcome

- **Intro cards on first launch.** Four short illustrated cards explain the cube, the slice rule, how to turn and tap, and how to look inside. Swipe through them or skip.
- **Practice cube.** After the cards you can try a small guided cube that walks you through turning, selecting, placing digits, slice view, notes and hints.
- You can see the cards again any time from **How to play** on the menu.

### A simpler menu

- The home screen now has one big button: **Play**, or **Continue** when you have a game going.
- Play opens a **Choose a puzzle** page with two clear cards, Latin Cube and Slice Sudoku, each saying what it is. Pick a difficulty and tap Start.
- The daily puzzle is its own card and shows your streak. How to play, Stats and Settings are small links at the bottom.

### Hints that teach

Hint no longer just fills a cell. The first tap picks a cell and tells you **why** it works, for example "5 has only one spot left in this Z slice", and switches slice view to that slice so you can check. Tap Hint again or **Fill in** to place it. If you made a mistake somewhere, the hint points to it first.

### Easier to read

- Each number on the pad shows **how many are still missing**.
- The top bar shows your **mistake count**.
- Cells with the same digit as the one you selected get a warm highlight.
- **X, Y and Z letters** float next to the cube in the same colors as the slice buttons, so you always know which way a slice runs. The focused slice gets an outline in that color, and the rest fade further out of the way.
- A new **win screen** with your time front and center, a "New best time" badge, mistakes, hints and your streak, plus a Next puzzle button.

### More fun

- The cube builds itself cell by cell when a puzzle starts.
- **Confetti** when you solve it.

### Installing

1. Download `3d-sudoku-1.1.0.apk` below on your Android phone.
2. Open it and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps if Android asks.
3. Tap Install.

**Coming from 1.0.0?** This build is signed with a different key, so Android will refuse to install it over 1.0.0. Uninstall 1.0.0 first, then install this one. Uninstalling clears saved progress and stats. Future versions can update in place once a permanent signing key is set up.

Needs Android 7.0 or newer.
