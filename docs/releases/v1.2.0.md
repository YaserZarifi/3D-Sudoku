## 3D Sudoku 1.2.0

A polish update focused on how the game looks and reads.

### New typography

- A new display font, **Outfit**, for titles, big numbers, the number pad and the digits on the cube. It's paired with Inter for everything else.
- Clearer contrast between sizes, tighter spacing on big text and small spaced-out labels for sections, so screens are easier to scan.
- Given digits on the cube are bold and dark, your own entries are lighter and blue, so you can tell them apart at a glance.

### A proper stats screen

Stats now opens as its own page instead of a plain popup:

- Three headline numbers at the top: puzzles solved, win rate and your daily streak.
- A card for each tier with Easy, Medium and Hard side by side, showing solved games, a win-rate bar, and your best and average times.
- A daily card with your current streak, longest streak and dailies solved.

### Fixes

- Digits no longer spill outside their cell while you turn the cube. They now scale with the cell and stay inside it at every angle, including in slice view.
- The daily puzzle card text fits on small screens.

### Installing

1. Download `3d-sudoku-1.2.0.apk` below on your Android phone.
2. Open it and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps if Android asks.
3. Tap Install.

**Updating from an earlier version?** Each build so far is signed with its own key, so uninstall the old version first. That clears saved progress and stats. This goes away once a permanent signing key is set up for releases.

Needs Android 7.0 or newer.
