## 3D Sudoku 1.0.0

The first public build. Sudoku, but the grid is a cube you can turn in your hand. Everything runs offline: no ads, no account, no internet permission.

### Two ways to play

- **Latin Cube** (digits 1 to 3). Every line through the cube holds 1, 2 and 3 once. Small, quick and a good way to learn how to read a cube.
- **Slice Sudoku** (digits 1 to 9). Every flat slice of the cube, along x, y and z, holds 1 to 9 once. This is the main game.

Each tier has Easy, Medium and Hard. Every puzzle is generated on your phone and checked to have exactly one solution, and none of them need guessing.

### What's inside

- **Daily puzzle.** One shared Slice Sudoku every day, with a streak counter.
- **Pencil marks.** Turn on Notes to jot down candidates. When you place a digit, it clears itself from the notes in that cell's lines and slices.
- **How to play.** A short guided first game that teaches turning the cube, reading lines, slices and notes.
- **Slice view.** Tap X, Y or Z to show one layer at a time, so the hidden middle cell is easy to reach.
- **Undo, Erase and Hint.** Undo covers everything, notes included.
- **Statistics.** Games played, solved, best and average times for every tier and level, plus your daily streak.
- **Autosave.** Close the app at any point and pick up where you left off.
- **Light and dark themes**, sound and haptics toggles, and a Reduced motion option.

### Installing

1. Download `3d-sudoku-1.0.0.apk` below on your Android phone.
2. Open it. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps.
3. Tap Install.

Needs Android 7.0 or newer. Works best on phones with Vulkan support, and falls back to OpenGL on older ones.

### Known limits

- This build has only been tested on desktop and in emulated input, not on a wide range of phones yet. If something feels off, please open an issue with your phone model.
- Releases built before a permanent signing key is set up may need an uninstall before installing a newer version. Your progress is stored on the phone, so uninstalling also clears it.
